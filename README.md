One joke about Monday mornings, 200 characters at most, judged on laughs. Free to enter, $5 reward, entries open until 26 Nov.

## The joke

> I told my boss I was running late because of Monday traffic. I was in my kitchen.

The setup sounds like the most ordinary Monday-morning excuse in the world — traffic — and the punchline pulls the rug out: there was no traffic at all, because the narrator never left the house. It is really about a Monday morning, the turn lands in one sentence, and it skips every tired Monday cliché (no alarm, no coffee, no "I hate Mondays").

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` validates the joke is 1–200 characters and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, the artifact the house judge compares two at a time.

## Candidates considered

Ten fresh jokes were written and scored against the judge's brief in its published order (about Monday mornings first, then a punchline that lands, surprise, economy of words, freshness). The winner above scored highest: it is unmistakably a Monday-morning joke, its twist is not the first thing anyone would think of, and every word does work at 81 characters.
